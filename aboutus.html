What we do

At HappyPaws, we provide a safe, nurturing haven for abandoned, surrendered, and stray dogs. Our day-to-day work involves rescuing dogs in need, providing them with essential veterinary care, and rehabilitating them through positive reinforcement training. We spend our days getting to know every dog’s unique personality so we can actively match them with loving families who are ready to give them the life they deserve.

History

HappyPaws began as a grassroots effort by a small group of local dog lovers who saw a growing need in our community. What started as a modest network of foster homes quickly grew into a full-scale rescue operation. Thanks to the overwhelming support of our volunteers and local partners, we were eventually able to open our dedicated shelter space, allowing us to house, care for, and ultimately save hundreds of dogs over the years.

Our mission is simple: to make sure every dog knows what it feels like to be loved. We are committed to reducing the number of homeless dogs through rescue, rehabilitation, and community education.

We strive to be a no-kill organization that not only advocates for animal welfare but also fosters lifelong bonds between humans and their canine companions.

We want our adoptions to be successful for both you and your new best friend. Our process is designed to be thorough but straightforward:

  1. Apply: Browse our available dogs and submit an online adoption application.
  2. Meet & Greet: If your application is a good match, we'll invite you (and your current pets) to the shelter to meet the dog in person.
  3. Counseling: We will sit down with you to discuss the dog's specific needs, behavioral quirks, and medical history.
  4. Take Them Home: Once everything is approved, you'll sign the adoption paperwork, receive a starter care package, and welcome your new family member

We rely entirely on adoption fees, community donations, local business sponsors, and volunteer support to fund our daily operations.
